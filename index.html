OtthonRadar Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

OtthonRadar is a map-based real estate search application for Android devices. It helps users discover properties for sale, properties for rent, and holiday accommodation, primarily in Hungary.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, processed, and shared when you use OtthonRadar.

No account required

OtthonRadar does not require users to create an account or sign in to search for properties.

Location information

OtthonRadar may request access to your device's location while the application is in use.

Location information is used to center the map, identify nearby properties, determine search areas, and calculate approximate distances.

Users can deny or revoke location permission in their Android device settings. The map may also be navigated manually.

Depending on the features used, geographic coordinates or selected search areas may be transmitted to map, geocoding, or external property search services.

OtthonRadar is not intended to continuously track users' locations in the background.

Internet access and property searches

OtthonRadar requires an internet connection to retrieve online maps and property listings.

Search requests may include the selected geographic area, search radius, property type, and other search parameters.

These requests may be sent to external property websites and search services. Such services may process technical information, including IP addresses, according to their own privacy policies.

Maps and geocoding

OtthonRadar uses map data associated with OpenStreetMap and may use third-party map tile and geocoding services.

Map and location-related requests may disclose the requested geographic area and technical network information to relevant providers.

OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

Advertising and Google AdMob

OtthonRadar displays advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information, including device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information.

Such information may be used for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and other purposes described in Google's policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Advertising Technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Locally stored information

OtthonRadar may save application preferences on the user's device, including selected search settings and display preferences.

Locally stored application data can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application.

Third-party websites and links

Property search results may include information and links from external websites.

When users open an external listing, the destination website's terms and privacy policy apply. OtthonRadar does not control the data practices of those websites.

Data retention

Locally stored application preferences may remain on the device until removed by the user.

Information processed by external providers may be retained according to their respective privacy and retention policies.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are intended to protect information processed through the application. However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your privacy choices

Users may control location permissions through Android settings, manage available advertising privacy controls, and remove local application data by clearing storage or uninstalling OtthonRadar.

Depending on applicable law, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, restriction, and objection to the processing of personal data.

Children's privacy

OtthonRadar is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features, third-party services, or data practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page, with an updated revision date where appropriate.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the OtthonRadar developer using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play store listing.
